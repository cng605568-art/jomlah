# v1.0.6 (BETA): Anak panah lorong, suara keluar lebih pandai
**Kemas kini biasa: pasang terus atas v1.0.5, tak perlu buang yang lama.**

### Baharu
- 🛣️ **Anak panah lorong** di bawah kad belokan, macam Waze dan Google Maps. Lorong yang patut anda guna diterangkan. Ada di lebih kurang separuh susur keluar dan simpang lebuh raya sekitar KL (di mana OpenStreetMap ada tanda lorong).
- 🗣️ Suara sebut **nombor exit dan nama persimpangan**: "keluar kiri di exit 2 0 9, UPM, ke arah Serdang".
- 🔁 Bila dua belokan dekat-dekat, suara terus beritahu: "Lepas tu terus ambil kiri."
- ⭕ Di bulatan, suara beritahu jalan keluar yang mana: "Ambil jalan keluar yang kedua."
- 🧪 **Simulasi** kini letak kamera dan Abang Polis latihan di depan, supaya anda boleh dengar amaran suara tanpa kereta. Laju simulasi terakhir juga diingat.

---

**Regular update: install straight over v1.0.5, no need to uninstall.**

### New
- 🛣️ **Lane arrows** under the turn card, like Waze and Google Maps. The lane to be in is highlighted. Available at about half the highway exits and forks around KL (where OpenStreetMap has lane markings).
- 🗣️ The voice says the **exit number and interchange name**: "exit 2 0 9, UPM, towards Serdang".
- 🔁 When two turns come close together, the voice tells you both: "Then keep left."
- ⭕ At roundabouts, the voice says which exit to take: "Take the second exit."
- 🧪 **Simulasi** now puts a practice speed camera and police alert on the road ahead, so you can hear the alert voices without a car. It also remembers the last speed you picked.
