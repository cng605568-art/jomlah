# v1.0.9 (BETA): Kereta lebih stabil · 车子更稳 · Steadier car
**Kemas kini biasa: pasang terus atas versi lama, tak perlu buang.**

### Dibaiki
- 🚗 Kereta tak lagi melompat-lompat atau bergetar bila memandu perlahan dekat destinasi.
- 🛣️ Bila memandu perlahan dekat U-turn atau pusing blok, kereta dan garisan biru kekal atas jalan yang betul, bukan di sebelah jalan.
- 📍 Bila sampai, kereta kekal atas jalan semasa parkir.
- ➰ Kalau tersasar jalan, garisan biru tak lagi lalu terus tembus rumah. Garisan titik-titik tunjuk jalan balik ke laluan.

**Regular update: install straight over the old version, no need to uninstall.**

### Fixed
- 🚗 The car no longer jumps around or twitches when driving slowly near your destination.
- 🛣️ Driving slowly near a U-turn or around a block, the car and the blue line stay on the right road, not beside it.
- 📍 After you arrive, the car stays on the road while you park.
- ➰ If you take a wrong turn, the blue line no longer cuts straight through houses. A dotted line shows the way back to the route.
